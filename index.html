Problem and challenge
  • This company has many competitors in same field, the primary challenge is to differentiate itself from competitors with product design and to break through in this highly competitive market.
  • In the absence of physical stores, they need to convince customers to transact with them online , 2D images are still far insufficient.
  • Most e-commerce websites have complicated user login system and checkout process, which causes inconvenienc and even leads to customers churn.
  • Some customers complain about that they can hardly find product they want, even though they already know that the product name, but they still struggling from find what exactly they want.
My approach
  • Competitor analysis
  • Information architecture
  • Intellegent search autocomplete system
  • Sign in with Google account
  • Interactive 3D content
  • Streamlined check out process
Competitor analysis
I worked closely with product manager to conduct the competitor analysis, we had listed some direct and indirect competitors in the same field on an analysis speadsheet.
This spreadsheet is to show stakeholders what we can learn from them, what we ought to avoid, how we can differentiate ourselves, and how we can enter the market.
Information architecture
In order to integrate a smooth structure, I had run a card sorting to organize the requirements, which allow us to build information architecture which is intuitive to navigate through without risking any confusion from the customers.
Intellegent search autocomplete system
In additional to the optimized information architecture, we still need a search system for customers to find what they want quickly.
I had implemented an intelligent search filtering system, customers can find what exactly they want without typing the full but lengthy keywords.
Customers can easily understand if what they want is on the list, which can significantly save their time.
Sign in with Google account
Our memory is pretty limited, and it is never easy to memorize multiple usernames and passwords used to sign in to a platforms.
Therefore, reducing cognitive load for customers is necessary and shall be prioritized, because every one has at least one Google account, so this is why I propose to integrate Google account sign in feature.
After the website was launched, the result of data analysis shows that more than 70% of customers are satisfied with this design.
Interactive 3D content
Through competitor analysis, I find out that most e-commerce websites, whether direct or indirect competitors, they showcase their product details with photos.
This approach seems normal, but it also brings another problem. When consumers view photos, they can only zoom in to see details, and cannot learn more details by rotating the photos 360 degrees.
This is what interactive 3D contents can do, with it, customers can zoom and rotate the 3d content 360 degrees. Customers can draw and sheath the katana by sliding the slider and the thumb the learn more detailed info.
The Streamlined check out process
The complicated check out process is always annoying for everyone, so it must be Streamlined.
I propose to reduce some unnecessary forms and multiple stages of process with few forms such as full name, complete address, and the phone number, in order to reduce the cognitive load for customers.
After discussion with stakeholders, they find that this proposal is technically feasible.
